Privacy policy

Clear information about data, accounts and platform activity.

This prototype page shows the intended structure. Production legal text requires review for the final business and jurisdictions.

Information collected

Account, profile, enquiry, transaction and usage information should be collected only where it supports a defined product purpose.

How information is used

Production implementation should document authentication, messaging, payments, analytics, moderation, support and legal compliance separately.

Control and retention

Users should be able to update account details, manage preferences and request account deletion subject to legitimate retention obligations.
