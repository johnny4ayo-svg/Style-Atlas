# Core User Flows

## Customer finds and contacts a designer

Homepage search to directory results to filters to profile comparison to designer profile to quote request to account sign-in to enquiry confirmation to message thread.

## Customer books a consultation

Profile availability to consultation details to date selection to account sign-in to Paystack checkout when paid to booking confirmation to reminders.

## Customer buys a product

Marketplace to product detail to variant choice to cart to address to shipping method to Paystack checkout to order confirmation to account order status.

## Fashion business joins

Add Your Business to account creation to role selection to business details to category and city to portfolio upload to services and pricing to verification explanation to plan selection to preview to submission for moderation.

## Business handles a lead

Business dashboard to new enquiry to customer brief to quote response to message thread to consultation or project status to completion to review request.

## School receives an enquiry

School directory to school profile to course detail to admission enquiry to school dashboard to response and follow-up.

## Employer posts a job

Jobs to Post a Job to employer sign-in to job form to package or payment to moderation to published role to applications dashboard.

## Organiser promotes an event

Events to Submit an Event to organiser sign-in to event form to media and sessions to promotion selection to Paystack to moderation to published event.

## Administrator verifies a business

Admin verification queue to application detail to identity and business evidence to decision with reason to audit log to business notification.
