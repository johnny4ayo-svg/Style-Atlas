# STYLEATLAS Brand Guidelines

## Positioning

STYLEATLAS is Nigeria's premium fashion map. It combines trusted professional discovery, editorial context and useful business tools in one connected platform.

## Logo concept

The monogram combines an S-shaped movement line, an A-shaped structure and a compass rose. The compass references discovery without turning the logo into a literal map pin.

Use the horizontal logo in headers and communications. Use the circular monogram for app icons, avatars and favicons.

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the diameter of the small centre compass shape. Do not place the logo against busy photography without a controlled dark or light field.

## Minimum size

- Horizontal digital logo: 180 px wide
- Monogram digital icon: 28 px square
- Print horizontal logo: 40 mm wide

## Colour

- Obsidian: `#080807`
- Deep charcoal: `#12110F`
- Warm ivory: `#F7F2E9`
- Champagne gold: `#C69A52`
- Burnished bronze: `#8A6032`
- Deep emerald: `#174C3C`

Champagne gold is an accent, not a full-page effect. Use it for emphasis, borders, active states and editorial detail.

## Typography

Display: Playfair Display in this prototype. A licensed fashion editorial serif can replace it later.

Interface and body: Manrope.

## Photography

Prioritise Nigerian and African fashion context, realistic skin and fabric texture, controlled lighting, strong cropping and varied composition. Avoid plastic faces, distorted hands, low-resolution imagery and repeated models across unrelated businesses.

## Voice

Write with precision. Explain services, prices, timelines and decisions in language a customer can use. Avoid vague luxury clichés.
