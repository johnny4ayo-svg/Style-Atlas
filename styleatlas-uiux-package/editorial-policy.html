Editorial policy

Independent stories, clear corrections and labelled commercial relationships.

Editorial judgement should remain separate from verification, ratings and paid placement.

Selection and sourcing

Stories should use named sources, original reporting where possible and clear language around uncertainty or opinion.

Commercial disclosure

Sponsored material, affiliate relationships and hosted events should be labelled where a reasonable reader can see the disclosure.

Corrections

Material errors should be corrected promptly, with a visible note when the change affects the meaning of the story.
