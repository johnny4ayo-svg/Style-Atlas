Terms of use

The rules that keep discovery, transactions and participation understandable.

This prototype outlines structure only. Final terms require qualified legal review.

Platform role

STYLEATLAS provides discovery, profile, communication and commerce tools. The production terms should clearly define when the platform is a marketplace operator and when it is not a party to a service agreement.

User responsibilities

Users should provide accurate information, respect intellectual property, follow review standards and avoid misleading claims.

Business participation

Listings, verification, subscriptions and promotional placements require distinct policies and enforcement procedures.
